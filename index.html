About Find Love

Find Love is a simple, privacy-focused notes app that lets you create, edit, and view your personal notes right on your device.

The app works completely offline by default and only uses your internet connection when needed for syncing notes or loading basic app content. No login, sign-up, or account creation is required.

Data Collection

Find Love does NOT collect any personal data. Your notes and information stay completely private on your device.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Photos, media, or files
  • Precise location
  • Device identifiers or IP address
  • Login/account details

There is no user account system and no data sharing with third parties because no personal data is collected.

Permissions & Internet Usage

The app only uses internet for essential functions like syncing notes across devices or loading app updates. No personal data is ever transmitted to the developer.

Find Love does NOT request access to:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Storage (personal files)
  • Location services
  • SMS or call logs

Advertisements & Tracking

The app is 100% ad-free with no banner ads, interstitial ads, or rewarded videos.

  • No third-party SDKs or ad networks
  • No user tracking or analytics services
  • No profiling or behavioral advertising
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Children's Privacy / Age Limitation

Find Love contains no harmful or inappropriate content and is safe for all ages. However, the app is intended for users aged 13 and above.

Since no personal data is collected, there are no additional privacy risks for younger users, though parental guidance is recommended.

Internal Statistics

The developer may collect completely anonymous, non-personal statistics like total number of notes created across all users or generic app usage counts.

These statistics:

  • Cannot identify any individual user
  • Are only visible to the developer
  • Are used solely to improve app quality

Users should review this page periodically to stay informed about any changes.
